# Promote an event

Work in the event's folder. Read `event-plan.md` first, plus `brand-voice.md` and `offers.md`.

## Files to write

1. **`landing-page.md`:** headline (the outcome), 3 things they'll learn, who it's for, date, time and format, speaker bio, FAQ (cost, recording, length), one call to action. Under 400 words.
2. **`promo-schedule.md`:** a dated table counting down from 4 weeks: channel, message, asset, owner, status. Default: announcement, weekly social posts, 2 invite emails, "last chance" post, reminders at 1 week, 1 day and 1 hour.
3. **`emails.md`:**
   - Invite (to the list)
   - Confirmation (after sign-up)
   - Reminders: 1 week, 1 day, 1 hour
   - Follow-up for attendees: thanks, recording, the offer
   - Follow-up for no-shows: recording, one reason to watch, the offer
4. **`content-request.md`** for the content manager: blog post that supports the event topic, 3–5 social posts, newsletter mention, each with a deadline taken from `promo-schedule.md`.

## Rules

Drafts only: never send or schedule anything. Speakers and partners named only if confirmed. Every fact checked by web search. Update `status` in `event-plan.md` to `promoting` when the promo is ready.
