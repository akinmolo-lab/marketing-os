# Write an article

## Steps

1. **Need a brief.** Find the brief in `04-content/drafts/`. If there isn't one, run `content-brief` first.
2. **Read** `01-foundation/brand-voice.md` and 2–3 past posts in `library/content/past-blogs/` to match the voice.
3. **Write** to `04-content/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` using this structure:
   - **Title:** contains the main question or its answer.
   - **Opening (2–3 sentences):** the direct answer. No scene-setting.
   - **H2s as questions** people ask, each answered in its first sentence.
   - **Short paragraphs** (2–4 sentences), one idea each. Use lists and tables for steps and comparisons.
   - **Proof:** customer examples (anonymised unless approved) and statistics with named sources.
   - **FAQ section:** 3–5 related questions with 1–3 sentence answers.
   - **Call to action** from `offers.md`.
   - **Internal links** to 2–3 past posts.
4. **SEO details** at the bottom of the draft: meta title (under 60 characters), meta description (under 155), URL slug, suggested FAQ schema.
5. **Before publishing checklist:** every fact with its source, anything marked `[CHECK]`, images needed.
6. Update the calendar row to status `draft`.

## Rules

Facts by web search only, never memory. Follow the brand voice and its banned words. Never publish.
