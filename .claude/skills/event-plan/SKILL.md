# Plan an event

## If asked for the yearly calendar

Propose 12 themes in the "Monthly event themes" table in `02-strategy/marketing-plan.md`: one per month, each tied to a goal and a persona, mixing formats. Avoid August and late December for big events unless the audience suggests otherwise. Ask before writing.

## For one event

1. **Theme.** Use the month's theme from the marketing plan. If none, propose 3 options (topic, format, persona, goal) and recommend one.
2. **Date and time.** Check it isn't a UK bank holiday or clashing with a major industry event (web search). Pick a time that suits the persona (e.g. evenings for owner-operators).
3. **Folder.** Copy `03-campaigns/events/_event-template/` to `03-campaigns/events/YYYY-MM-<short-name>/` and fill in `event-plan.md`.
4. **Run-of-show** (`run-of-show.md`): minute by minute. Default 45–60 minutes: welcome, the teaching (3 points), live example, Q&A, call to action, close.
5. **Checklist** (`checklist.md`):
   - 6 weeks: theme, date, speaker confirmed by a person, platform or venue booked by a person
   - 4 weeks: landing page live, registration open
   - 1 week: slides done, tech test
   - Day before: reminder, run-through
   - Day of: 1-hour reminder, recording on
   - Within 24 hours: follow-up with recording and offer
   - 1 week after: results to the analyst
6. **Goal.** Set registration, attendance and lead targets from past events in `library/campaigns/` (say which ones). With no history, mark targets as first guesses.

## Output

The folder path, the plan summary, and the tasks a person must do (bookings, speakers) with dates.
