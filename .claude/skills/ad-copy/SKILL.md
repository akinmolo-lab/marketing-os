# Write the ads

Work in the campaign folder. Read `brief.md` and `ads-plan.md` first, plus `brand-voice.md`, `offers.md`, `ideal-customer.md`, and past winning ads in `library/campaigns/`.

## Before writing

**Check current ad specs and limits by web search** for each platform in the plan (character limits, image and video sizes, number of headlines). Note the source and date at the top of `ad-copy.md`. Defaults to check against:

| Platform | Format | Usual limits (verify) |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search | Responsive search ad | Up to 15 headlines of 30 characters; up to 4 descriptions of 90 characters |
| Meta | Image or video ad | Primary text, headline and description; keep the key message in the first 125 characters of primary text |
| LinkedIn | Single image ad | Introductory text, headline; keep the key message in the first 150 characters |

## Angles

Write 3 angles per ad set, each tied to something real in `ideal-customer.md`:

1. **Pain:** the problem in the customer's own words.
2. **Outcome:** the result they get, specific and provable.
3. **Proof:** a real review, result or credential (from `library/customers/reviews.md` or `offers.md`; anonymised).

## Write `ad-copy.md`

For each ad set: the angle, every text field with its character count, the call-to-action button, the destination URL with UTMs, and a **creative brief** (what the image or video shows, text on image, size). For Google, write enough headlines and descriptions to fill the ad, and suggest which to pin, if any.

Add a **landing page brief**: headline matching the ad, the one offer, proof, form fields (as few as possible), and the thank-you step. If the page needs writing, put it in `content-request.md` for `content-manager`.

## Rules

- **Under the limit, every field.** Count the characters; show the count.
- **No claims we can't prove.** No "best", "guaranteed", "#1" or numbers without a source. UK ASA / CAP Code: prices must be clear and include VAT where it applies for consumers.
- **Platform policy.** Avoid personal attributes in copy ("Are you struggling with debt?") that Meta and others reject; flag anything that may need a special ad category.
- **Offer matches `offers.md` exactly.**
- Drafts only. End by suggesting `/qa` on `ad-copy.md`.
