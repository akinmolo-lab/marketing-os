# Campaign or event analysis

## Steps

1. **Goal.** Read the folder's `brief.md` (campaign) or `event-plan.md` (event) for the goal and KPI targets.
2. **Data.** Find the results in `06-measurement/data/` or connectors. For events: registrations, attendees, attendance rate, questions asked, follow-up replies, leads, sales. List anything missing as `[MISSING]` and ask for it.
3. **Compare.** Target vs actual, and vs similar past campaigns or events in `library/campaigns/` (name them).
4. **Explain.** What worked, what didn't, and the likely reasons, with evidence. Separate what the data shows from your judgement.
5. **Lessons.** 3 lessons for next time, phrased as instructions ("Open registration 4 weeks out, not 2").
6. **Write** the results into the folder's `results.md` (campaign) or `report.md` (event).
7. **Suggest filing** it in the library with `/add-campaign`, and any experiment worth logging in `06-measurement/experiments/experiments-log.md`.

## Rules

Real numbers only, each with its source. No personal data: counts and anonymised quotes only.
