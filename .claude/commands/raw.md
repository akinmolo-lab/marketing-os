---
description: Sort the inbox. Turns raw notes into structured files in the right folders, on confirmation.
---

Process everything in `inbox/` (ignore `.gitkeep`).

1. Read each file. Work out what it is: customer insight, campaign idea, content idea, competitor note, data, decision or task.
2. Propose a routing plan as a table: file, what it is, destination, what changes (new file or appended section).
   - Customer insights → `library/customers/interviews/` (anonymise names first)
   - Content ideas → `04-content/content-calendar.md` (status `idea`)
   - Competitor notes → `library/competitors/`
   - Campaign ideas → the relevant campaign brief, or flag for `/new-campaign`
   - Decisions → `07-operations/decisions-log.md`
3. Wait for my yes. Then file everything, appending rather than overwriting. Delete an inbox original only after confirming it was filed.
