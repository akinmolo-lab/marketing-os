Create a campaign for: $ARGUMENTS

1. Ask me (in one message) for anything you can't infer: goal and KPI, audience, offer, dates, channels, budget.
2. Copy `03-campaigns/_campaign-template/` to `03-campaigns/YYYY-MM-<short-name>/`.
3. Fill in `brief.md`, using `01-foundation/` for audience and message, and `library/campaigns/` for lessons from similar past campaigns (mention them in the brief).
4. Draft the assets table and task list with due dates.
5. Add the content pieces to `04-content/content-calendar.md` with status `idea`.
