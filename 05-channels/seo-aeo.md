## AEO checklist (applied by the content manager)

- Answer the main question in the first 2–3 sentences.
- Use question-style headings that match how people ask.
- Short, quotable paragraphs; one fact or step each.
- An FAQ section for related questions.
- Named sources for every statistic.
- Clear author, date and "last updated".
- Consistent company name and description everywhere online.
